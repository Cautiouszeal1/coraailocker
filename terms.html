02Description of Services

CoraAiVault provides a decentralised token and liquidity locking platform on BNB Smart Chain. Our services include:

Token Locking: The ability to lock BEP-20 tokens in immutable smart contracts for a specified duration.

Liquidity Locking: The ability to lock PancakeSwap LP tokens in immutable smart contracts.

Vesting Schedules: Creation of multi-tranche token vesting arrangements via separate on-chain locks.

Contract Scanner: An automated tool that analyses token contracts for common risk indicators. This is provided for informational purposes only and does not constitute a professional audit or security guarantee.

Public Lock Explorer: A publicly accessible tool for viewing locked tokens associated with any wallet address.

Lock Certificates: Shareable digital records of completed locks.

All locking operations are executed through smart contracts on the BNB Smart Chain blockchain. Once tokens are locked, they cannot be retrieved until the specified unlock date — by anyone, including CoraAiVault.

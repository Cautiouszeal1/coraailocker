Section 02
The Problem

The cryptocurrency industry loses billions of dollars annually to fraudulent projects, exit scams, and rug pulls. Despite years of growth, the fundamental infrastructure for verifying project legitimacy remains shockingly primitive.

💸
Rug Pulls & Exit Scams
Projects raise millions, lock nothing, then drain liquidity and disappear. Investors have no mechanism to verify that funds or tokens are committed long-term before investing.
🎭
Fake Liquidity Locks
Some projects claim to lock liquidity using fake or reversible mechanisms. Without an immutable, verifiable locker, investors cannot distinguish real locks from fabricated screenshots.
👁️
Hidden Team Wallets
Teams hold unlocked tokens in wallets investors don't know about. At launch, these wallets dump on retail buyers, causing immediate price collapse and destroying investor capital.
📉
No Trust Infrastructure
There is no universal standard for verifying project legitimacy. Each investor must manually research contracts, wallets, and lock status — a process most lack the skills to perform correctly.

The Scale: According to on-chain analytics, over $3 billion was lost to crypto scams and rug pulls in 2023 alone. The vast majority of victims had no reliable tools to verify the projects they invested in before losing their funds.

Section 11
Security Architecture

Security is the entire product at CoraAiVault — not a feature. Every technical decision prioritizes security first. Our smart contracts are designed to be trustless by construction, meaning users never need to trust us — they only need to trust the code.

🔐
Immutable User Funds
Once tokens are locked, no account — including the contract owner address — can access, move, or modify them before the unlock date. This is enforced at the EVM level with no override mechanism possible.
🔑
Owner Role Isolation
The owner address has exactly two permissions: collect platform fees and update the fee amount. It has zero ability to interact with user funds under any circumstances. Owner key compromise cannot harm users.
🛡️
Re-Entrancy Protection
All withdrawal functions use the checks-effects-interactions pattern, marking locks as withdrawn before executing token transfers. This prevents all known re-entrancy attack vectors at the contract level.
📋
Professional Audit
A comprehensive smart contract audit by a top-tier firm (Certik, SolidProof, or Hacken) is planned for Phase 2. The full audit report will be published publicly and linked directly from the platform.
🏦
Multi-Sig Treasury
All platform revenue is held in a multi-signature wallet requiring multiple key holders to approve any transaction. No single compromised key can drain platform funds.
♾️
Permanent Operation
CoraAiVault's smart contracts run on the BSC blockchain permanently. Even if the entire team disappeared tomorrow, every lock would still release to its rightful owner on schedule. The code runs forever.

Zero-Trust Design Principle: CoraAiVault is built so that trusting us is unnecessary. Every commitment is enforced by immutable code. Every lock is publicly verifiable. Every transaction is permanently recorded. The platform's integrity does not depend on our honesty — it depends on mathematics.

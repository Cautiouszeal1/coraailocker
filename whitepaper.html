01 — Vision
Built to fix
a broken system.

Billions of dollars leave crypto investors' wallets every year through rug pulls and exit scams. The cause is always the same: investors commit capital based on promises, not proof. By the time they realise the promises were empty, the funds are gone.

CoraAiVault started with one question: what if the lock itself was the proof? Not a screenshot. Not a tweet. An actual smart contract on a public blockchain holding tokens until a specific date, verifiable by any investor in ten seconds from anywhere in the world.

🌎
Vision
A world where every crypto project can prove its legitimacy instantly. Where trust is enforced by code, not words, and investors verify commitments in seconds rather than hours.
🎯
Mission
To become the standard trust verification layer for Web3 — the way SSL certificates became standard for websites. Every serious project locks on CoraAiVault. Every investor checks before buying.

Five-year goal: CoraAiVault operates across 6+ blockchains with AI-powered risk scoring, institutional vault services, and a fully decentralised governance system controlled by $CORA holders.

Cross-chain: Phase 3 deploys CoraAiVault on Ethereum, Base, Polygon, and Arbitrum. Phase 5 adds Solana. Same contract, same interface, six blockchains.

AI advantage: No existing locker scans contracts before you lock. We do. Real-time risk scoring, wallet behaviour analysis, and predictive rug alerts — features no competitor can quickly build.

02 — The Problem
Why investors
keep losing money.

Over $3 billion was lost to exit scams in 2023 alone. No advanced technology is required to steal from crypto investors — a convincing project page and an unlocked wallet is all it takes.

💸
Rug Pulls
Teams raise capital, lock nothing, then drain liquidity and disappear. Investors have no way to verify commitments before funds are gone.
🎭
Fake Locks
Projects claim to lock via screenshots or reversible mechanisms. Without an immutable on-chain record, there is no way to know what is real.
👁
Hidden Wallets
Teams hold unlocked tokens in wallets investors never see. At launch, those wallets dump on retail. Price collapses. Team disappears.
🔍
No Standard
There is no universal way to verify project commitments. Most investors lack the skills to check contracts, wallets, and locks manually.
03 — The Solution
Proof,
not promises.

CoraAiVault replaces promises with immutable on-chain records. Smart contracts hold tokens in a vault nobody — including us — can access before the unlock date. Every lock is public, permanent, and verifiable by anyone.

🔒
Immutable Locking
Once locked, tokens cannot be moved by anyone until the unlock date. No admin override. No backdoor. The code is the contract.
🔍
Full Transparency
Every lock is publicly visible on BSCScan. Anyone searches any wallet and sees what is locked, how much, and when it unlocks — in real time.
🤖
AI Risk Scanner
Built-in contract scanner flags dangerous functions before you lock or invest. Mint functions, blacklists, honeypot patterns — detected automatically.

08 — Deflationary Mechanisms
Supply goes
down over time.

The 5 billion $CORA supply is fixed and cannot increase. Three mechanisms actively reduce circulating supply as the platform grows.

🔥
Buyback & Burn
30% of all platform BNB revenue buys $CORA from the open market and permanently destroys it. Continuous buy pressure tied directly to usage.
💨
Fee Burns
A portion of every premium subscription paid in $CORA is burned rather than redistributed. Platform growth directly reduces supply.
📅
Quarterly Burns
Each quarter, $CORA holders vote on an additional burn event based on performance metrics. Community-controlled deflationary milestones.

Projected impact: Combined mechanisms are expected to reduce circulating supply by 15–25% within the first two years of full platform operation.

11 — Security
Security is the
whole product.
🔐
Immutable User Funds
Once locked, no account — including the contract owner — can access tokens before the unlock date. Enforced at the EVM level with no override mechanism.
🔑
Owner Role Isolation
The owner address can collect fees and update the fee amount. Zero ability to interact with user funds under any circumstances.
🛡
Re-Entrancy Protection
All withdrawal functions use checks-effects-interactions, marking locks as withdrawn before executing transfers. Prevents all known re-entrancy attacks.
📋
Professional Audit
A full audit by Certik, SolidProof, or Hacken is scheduled for Phase 2. The complete report will be published publicly and linked from the platform.
🏦
Multi-Sig Treasury
All platform revenue is held in a multi-signature wallet. Multiple key holders must approve every transaction. No single key can drain funds.
∞
Runs Forever
CoraAiVault contracts run on BSC permanently. If the team disappeared tomorrow, every lock would still release to its rightful owner on schedule.

Zero-trust design: CoraAiVault is built so trusting us is unnecessary. Every commitment is enforced by immutable code. The platform's integrity depends on mathematics, not our honesty.

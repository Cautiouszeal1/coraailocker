Every existing token locker provides the same basic service: hold tokens until a date. CoraAiVault goes further by adding intelligence to security. Our AI layer is the feature no competitor has built and most cannot replicate quickly.

🛡️ Security Architecture

Security is not a feature at CoraAiVault — it is the entire product. Every technical decision is made with security as the primary consideration. Our smart contracts are designed to be trustless by construction, meaning users never need to trust us — they only need to trust the code.

Immutable Locking: Once tokens are locked, no account — including the contract owner — can access or move them before the unlock date. This is enforced at the smart contract level with no override mechanism.

Owner Separation: The contract owner address can only collect platform fees and update the fee amount. It has zero ability to touch user funds under any circumstances.

🔐 Zero-Trust Design: CoraAiVault's smart contracts are written so that even if our entire team disappeared tomorrow, every lock would still release to its rightful owner on schedule. The code runs forever on the BSC blockchain.

Re-Entrancy Protection: All withdrawal functions follow the checks-effects-interactions pattern, marking locks as withdrawn before executing transfers to prevent re-entrancy attacks.

Audit Roadmap: A full professional smart contract audit by a top-tier firm (Certik, SolidProof, or Hacken) is planned for Phase 2. Audit reports will be published publicly in full.

OpenZeppelin Standards: Our contracts follow OpenZeppelin security patterns — the most battle-tested smart contract library in existence, securing billions of dollars across thousands of protocols.

Multi-Sig Treasury: All platform funds are held in a multi-signature wallet requiring multiple key holders to approve any transaction, eliminating single points of failure.

01 — Token & Liquidity Locking
Immutable locks that prove team commitment.

When a project locks tokens on CoraAiVault, those tokens are held in a smart contract until the unlock date. Nobody touches them before then — not the project, not us, not anyone with a multisig key.

Every lock is a public record on BSCScan. Investors search the contract address, see the lock, see the unlock date, and decide accordingly. No screenshots. No promises.

Security architecture
Trustless by design, not by policy.

The CoraAiVault smart contract is written so that trusting us is unnecessary. Every commitment is enforced by code on the BNB blockchain. If the entire team vanished tomorrow, every lock would still release to its owner on schedule.
